Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is the delivery? expand_more
Our delivery times typically range from 15 to 45 minutes, depending on your distance from the store and the complexity of your order.
Are the prices the same as in-store? expand_more
Yes! One of our core promises is "Real prices." We work directly with vendors to ensure the digital menu matches their in-store price tags.
Can I schedule a delivery for later? expand_more
Absolutely. You can choose to have your order delivered immediately or schedule it for any available time slot within the next 48 hours.
Is there a minimum order amount? expand_more
Minimum order amounts vary by vendor, but most have a very low threshold to encourage small, daily local purchases.
What if my item is out of stock? expand_more
Vendors update their stock in real-time. If an item becomes unavailable after ordering, you can choose between a refund or a substitution suggested by the merchant.
